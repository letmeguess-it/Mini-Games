Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026 · Applies to the Let Me Guess website at letmeguess-it.github.io/Mini-Games

The short version
  • Let Me Guess has no server of its own. Unless you join the global leaderboard, we never receive or see any of your data, and we never sell or share it.
  • Your game progress is stored in your browser. If you choose to sign in with Google, it's also saved in a hidden file in your own Google Drive that only this app can open.
  • The global leaderboard is optional. If you join, the display name you choose and your daily-challenge point totals become public. You can leave at any time, and your entries are deleted.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies.
  • You can delete everything at any time (see "Deleting your data").

1. Who we are

Let Me Guess is a free collection of guessing, estimation and logic games, published by the owner of the letmeguess-it GitHub account ("we", "us"). The website is a set of static files hosted on GitHub Pages; all game logic runs in your browser.

3. Signing in with Google (optional)

You can play without signing in. If you choose to sign in, Google asks you to allow these permissions:

  • See your name, email address and profile picture (openid, email, profile). These are used only to show who's signed in and to keep different people's progress apart on a shared device.
  • See, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). The app uses this to keep one file, let-me-guess-progress.json, in a hidden app-data folder. The app cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive, and the file doesn't appear in your normal Drive file list.

Your browser talks directly to Google. Your progress travels between your browser and your Google Drive and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy. Let Me Guess's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, Google user data is used only to provide the sign-in, progress-sync and optional leaderboard features described here. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not transferred to anyone except Firebase (Google) to run the leaderboard you chose to join.

Global leaderboard (optional)

Signed-in players can choose to join the global leaderboard. When you press Join:

  • You pick a display name. It defaults to your first name and last initial, and you can change it at any time. That name and your point totals are published on the public leaderboards. Your email address and profile picture are never shown.
  • Your browser uses your Google sign-in to create a sign-in record with Firebase Authentication, so that only you can update or delete your own entries.
  • Points are only your daily-challenge scores, calculated in your browser. No answers, guesses or other game details are sent.

Press Leave on the leaderboard at any time. This deletes your leaderboard entries and the sign-in record.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

Let Me Guess sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics, tracking pixels or advertising. It uses browser storage (described above) only to run the games and remember your progress.

5. Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when your browser requests the pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Firebase (Google Cloud) stores the optional leaderboard (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication). See Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Google provides sign-in and Drive storage if you choose to use them. Google's handling of your account is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. The Google sign-in script is only loaded when you press "Sign in with Google" or "Reconnect".

6. Deleting your data

  • Progress in your browser: clear this site's data in your browser settings (often under "Cookies and site data"). When signing out, you can also choose "Sign out and remove it here".
  • Progress in your Google Drive: open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps, find Let Me Guess, open Options and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • Your leaderboard entries: press Leave on the dashboard's leaderboard. Your entries and the leaderboard sign-in record are deleted.
  • The app's access to your Google account: remove Let Me Guess at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Apart from leaderboard entries (which Leave removes), we hold no copy of your data. If you can't use the Leave button, ask us through the contact link below and we'll remove your entries.

7. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Access tokens are short-lived and kept only in your browser's session storage, and the app asks Google for the narrowest Drive permission available. Anyone with access to your device and browser profile could see your game progress, so use "Sign out and remove it here" on shared computers.

8. Children

The games are suitable for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Signing in uses a Google account, which is subject to Google's own age requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

If the way the site handles data changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: open an issue at github.com/letmeguess-it/Mini-Games/issues.
